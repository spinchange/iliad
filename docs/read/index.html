The Iliad

a line-for-line translation · twenty-four books

  1. Book IThe plague; Agamemnon takes Achilles' prize, the girl Briseis, and Achilles quits the war; Thetis asks Zeus to avenge her son. ▶ 34 min
  2. Book IIZeus sends Agamemnon a false dream; Agamemnon tests the army and nearly loses it; the catalogue of the ships and their captains. ▶ 49 min
  3. Book IIIParis and Menelaus fight a duel for Helen; Helen names the Greek chiefs from the wall; Aphrodite carries Paris off to safety. ▶ 26 min
  4. Book IVThe gods break the truce with an arrow from Pandarus; Agamemnon reviews his troops; the first day of battle begins. ▶ 31 min
  5. Book VThe great day of Diomedes, who wounds two gods, Aphrodite and Ares, and drives them from the field. ▶ 51 min
  6. Book VIGlaucus and Diomedes exchange armor instead of blows; Hector in Troy with his mother Hecuba, with Helen, and with his wife Andromache. ▶ 29 min
  7. Book VIIHector and Ajax fight a duel to a draw; a truce to bury the dead; the Greeks build a wall around their ships.
  8. Book VIIIZeus forbids the gods to take part; the Trojans win the day and camp on the plain, their watchfires burning until dawn.
  9. Book IXThe embassy to Achilles: Agamemnon offers gifts and Briseis back; Odysseus, Phoenix, and Ajax plead with him, and he refuses.
  10. Book XA night raid: Odysseus and Diomedes catch the Trojan spy Dolon, kill King Rhesus in his sleep, and steal his horses.
  11. Book XIAgamemnon's day of glory; Diomedes, Odysseus, and other Greek chiefs are wounded one by one; Nestor sends Patroclus to plead with Achilles.
  12. Book XIIThe Trojans storm the Greek wall; Sarpedon tears down the rampart, and Hector smashes the gate with a boulder.
  13. Book XIIIWhile Zeus looks away, Poseidon rallies the Greeks in disguise, and the battle for the ships hangs in the balance.
  14. Book XIVHera seduces Zeus and lulls him to sleep; Poseidon turns the tide; Ajax fells Hector with a stone.
  15. Book XVZeus wakes and foretells the end of the war; Apollo levels the wall; Hector reaches the ships and calls for fire.
  16. Book XVIPatroclus fights in Achilles' armor, drives the Trojans from the ships, kills Sarpedon, and dies at Hector's hands.
  17. Book XVIIThe fight over the body of Patroclus; Hector puts on Achilles' armor; Achilles' immortal horses weep for the dead.
  18. Book XVIIIAchilles learns that Patroclus is dead and chooses vengeance over long life; Hephaestus forges him new armor and the great shield.
  19. Book XIXAchilles gives up his wrath against Agamemnon, who blames a goddess; Achilles arms for battle, and his horse foretells his death.
  20. Book XXZeus lets the gods into the fighting; Achilles returns to battle and meets Aeneas and Hector, whom the gods snatch to safety.
  21. Book XXIAchilles fills the river Scamander with the dead, and the river rises against him; the gods brawl; the Trojans flee inside the walls.
  22. Book XXIIHector waits alone outside the gates; Achilles chases him three times around the city and kills him; Priam, Hecuba, and Andromache mourn.
  23. Book XXIIIThe ghost of Patroclus asks for burial; his funeral; the games held in his honor, from the chariot race to the spear-throw.
  24. Book XXIVThe gods order Hector's body returned; Priam crosses the plain at night to ransom his son from Achilles; the burial of Hector.

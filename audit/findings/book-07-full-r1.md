## Summary

Book 7 — the Hector-Ajax duel, the burial truce, and the building of
the Achaean wall — was read entire against the Greek and produced the
audit's first firm finding: at 117 the concessive "insatiable of
battle" (μόθου ἀκόρητος) had been rendered "glutted with the grind of
battle," inverting the sense; fixed in this pass. Everything else
checked is exact: the sailors-and-following-wind opening simile; the
three Trojan kills with their anatomy; the Apollo-Athena compact by
the oak and the vulture-perch tableau (note [3]); Hector's challenge
with the mound-speech ("This is the mound of a man who died long
ago... and my fame will never perish"); Menelaus's rebuke with the
Thersites echo at 96 ("Women of Achaea, men of Achaea no longer" —
verified verbatim against 2.235) and the water-and-earth curse;
Agamemnon's restraint of his brother with αἴσιμα παρειπών rendered
identically to 6.62 ("for his urging was of things due"), the
cross-reference the commentary builds an argument on; Nestor's
Ereuthalion story complete (the iron club, the narrow lane, "youngest-
born of all"); the nine volunteers and the lot-procedure with its
marks-not-writing; Ajax's arming, the tower-shield excursus (Tychius,
seven hides, eighth of bronze), and the duel itself — where the
spear-exchange lines identical in Greek to 3.356-360 were mechanically
verified verbatim against the Book 3 English, as note [16] claims;
the heralds' "It is good to yield to night as well," repeated exactly
when Hector takes it up; the gift-exchange; the feast with the chine
and "no heart lacked a fair share of the feast" (consistent with the
4.48/24.69 harmonized formula); Nestor's burn-and-build counsel with
the athetized bones-to-the-children couplet; the Trojan assembly
(Antenor's motion, Paris's "the woman I will not give back," Priam's
relay — the truce-formula "until the god decides between us, and
hands victory to one side or the other" identical in all three
occurrences, 291-292/377-378/396-397, as in the Greek); Idaeus's
embassy and Diomedes' cables-of-destruction refusal; the shared
dawn field with the twin cremation couplets kept symmetrical
(428-429 = 431-432); the wall and ditch; the Poseidon-Zeus exchange
licensing the wall's erasure; the Lemnian wine-fleet with its
market-list ending in "slaves"; and the closing thunder with green
fear and the poured libations.

One below-floor observation for the register: εὐρυσθενές (455, of
Poseidon — "wide in strength") is rendered "wide-ruling," which
collides with "wide-ruling Agamemnon" for εὐρὺ κρείων (107, 322);
a one-off, no action urged. Notes spot-checked ([2], [5], [7], [9],
[12], [16], [17], [20], [22], [26], [27], [28]) are accurate,
including all athetesis claims. One fix applied; no escalation to r2.

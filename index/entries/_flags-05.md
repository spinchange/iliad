# Slice 05 — citation flags, reviewed

`tools/merge_index.py` flags any `book.line` an entry cites that is not an
occurrence of that headword. The check is advisory by design: an entry may
legitimately cite a scene line where the figure is present but named by
patronymic, epithet, periphrasis, or across an enjambment. Every flag raised
against `entries-05.md` was checked against the text on 2026-07-27 and is listed
here with its justification, so a later reviewer need not re-derive them.

One drafted claim was a **real error and has been fixed**: the Menelaus entry had
him kill the suppliant Adrestus at 6.63–65. He does not — Agamemnon shames him
out of taking the ransom, Menelaus pushes Adrestus back with his hand, and
Agamemnon stabs him in the flank (6.62–64). The kill was removed from the *Kills:*
list and the sentence rewritten. Two range-start refs were also tightened to the
attested line (Meriones 10.261→10.260; Menestheus, the Arcesilaus death cited at
15.331 rather than 15.329).
